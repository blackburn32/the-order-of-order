# The Order of Order tutorial

Each step is one callout. Unless a step lists a **Continue** trigger, it is
dismissed with the standard Continue button inside the callout.

Steps are remembered across runs. A run that ends partway through leaves the
rest for the next run, which skips every step already seen. The tutorial turns
itself off once every step has been seen, and turning Show Tutorial back on in
Settings replays it from step 1. The forced-1s safety net covers trial 1 only.

"Click/Press" and "scrolling/pinching" pick their word by platform: the phone
build says _Press_ and _pinching_, every other build _Click_ and _scrolling_.

## Trial overview (first visit)

### 1. Welcome young leader! Your order starts in its infancy at rank 1.

- Spotlight: the rank title

### 2. Complete 10 ranks to win the game.

- Spotlight: the rank title

### 3. Each rank is composed of three trials of increasing difficulty.

- Spotlight: the three trial cards

### 4. To complete a trial you must meet its goal by earning points.

- Spotlight: the goal on the first trial's card

### 5. Points are earned by rolling a 1 on any die.

- Spotlight: the goal on the first trial's card

### 6. Click/Press here to begin the first trial.

- Spotlight: the start button
- Continue: presses the start button

## The table

### 7. This is where your dice are shown.

- Spotlight: the dice grid

### 8. You can pan around by dragging.

- Spotlight: the dice grid
- Continue: pans around

### 9. And you can zoom in and out by scrolling/pinching.

- Spotlight: the dice grid
- Continue: zooms

### 10. Click/Press here to roll your dice.

- Spotlight: the roll button
- Continue: rolls

### 11. To score you must roll a 1, good luck.

- Spotlight: the dice grid
- Note: shown once the first roll settles. That roll never scores, so this
  step always lands before the trial can end.

## Trial results

### 12. Congratulations on completing your first trial!

- Spotlight: "Trial cleared" and the trial name / rank beneath it

### 13. After each trial you will receive gold which can be spent in the store!

- Spotlight: the gold earned and its full breakdown

### 14. You'll gain extra gold for completing a trial with rolls to spare.

- Spotlight: the receipt row paying gold for rolls left in hand
- Note: skipped if the first clear paid no rolls-left gold; shown instead on
  the first clear that does.

### 15. Your gold earns interest! 1 gold per 5 gold held at a trial's end, up to 5.

- Spotlight: the receipt row paying interest
- Note: skipped if the first clear paid no interest; shown instead on the first
  clear that does.

### 16. Click/Press here to enter the store.

- Spotlight: the Enter the Shop button
- Continue: presses the button
