Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The Order of Order is made by Adventure to Infinity LLC ("we"). This page covers the game in a browser, on itch.io, and on Android, and this website. The short version: the game has no accounts, collects no names, email addresses or locations, shows no ads, and sells nothing about you. It only sends anything to us if you post a run to the Global Hall, or if the game crashes.

What stays on your device

Your Hall of High Scores, unlocks, settings, statistics and any run in progress are saved in your browser's storage (or the app's storage on Android). They never leave your device unless you post a run or export your save yourself. You can remove them with Settings › Reset All Progress, or by clearing the site's data.

The Global Hall (opt-in)

The game asks before it posts anything to the shared leaderboard, and you can say no, or change your mind any time in Settings › Post to the Global Hall. If you post a run, we store:

  • a Board ID, a random code that identifies your device on the board (shown in Settings). It is made from a secret your device keeps, so nobody else can post as you;
  • the initials you enter (up to three letters);
  • the run's score, rank, character and medal, and its record: the rolls, purchases and choices in the run, so others can open its analysis and so we can check a score is real;
  • the version of the game that played it.

Your initials, score and run record are public: anyone viewing the Global Hall can see them. Your best run stays until you delete it or we reset the board. Daily Rite boards are deleted after 30 days.

To stop people flooding the board, the server counts submissions per connection for an hour at a time. It keeps a one-way hash of your IP address for that, changed every day, never the address itself, and deletes it within a day.

Crash reports

If the game hits an error it cannot recover from, it sends us the error message, where in the code it happened, which screen was open, the game version, the platform (web, itch.io or Android) and your browser's user agent. It sends no Board ID, initials or save data, and as with the board, the server keeps only a daily one-way hash of your IP address for rate limiting. Reports are kept for 30 days. You can turn this off in Settings › Send Crash Reports.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings › Delete My Board Record. That removes your Global Hall row, your Daily Rite rows and their run records straight away. If you no longer have the device, email privacy@the-order-of-order.com with your Board ID (or your initials and roughly when you posted), and we will delete it within 30 days.

Who processes it

The website, the leaderboard and the crash reports run on Cloudflare, which processes requests on our behalf. itch.io and Google Play have their own privacy policies for their storefronts. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has posted to the Global Hall, email us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Because the board holds no name or contact details, we identify your data by its Board ID. Email us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Contact: privacy@the-order-of-order.com.
