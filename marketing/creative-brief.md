# The Order of Order — Marketing Creative Brief

## Release frame

- Public status: free open beta
- Primary call to action: **Play the Open Beta**
- Current destination: <https://blackout32.itch.io/the-order-of-order>
- Primary surfaces: standalone website, itch.io, and a future Steam page
- Standalone domain: <https://the-order-of-order.com>
- Typical winning run: 15–20 minutes; longer for a first-time reader
- Spoiler boundary: Boss modifiers, late grids, and the King are safe. Never reveal
  the Order of Disorder or frame the King as openly evil.

## Positioning

**A dice-building incremental roguelike about teaching probability to sit still.**

The dice of the realm have never stopped rolling. The player discovers the
settling technique, founds a small order around it, and grows one humble d6 into
a screen-filling engine of sacred arithmetic. Runs climb 10 ranks of Lesser,
Greater, and Boss Trials through shops, build-defining relics, hostile modifiers,
and increasingly unreasonable score goals.

### Audience bridge

Lead with the readable buildcraft and run-defining choices familiar to players of
games such as Balatro. Differentiate with the escalating physical spectacle of the
dice grid and the mock-serious world of the Order.

### Core campaign line

> A sacred discipline. An unreasonable number of dice.

### Hero copy

**Teach dice to settle. Build an order. Defy probability.**

Grow one humble d6 into a screen-filling engine in a dice-building incremental
roguelike about sacred numbers, questionable doctrine, and increasingly
unreasonable trials.

Primary CTA: **Play the Open Beta**

Secondary CTA: **See How the Rite Works**

## Campaign pillars

### 1. The founding miracle

The realm's dice never stop turning. One die comes to rest for a moment and an
entire institution is built around the event.

The foundational story should ultimately be a separate animation: the future
founder tumbles down a hill while its face and expression change rapidly, then
comes to rest at the same instant it discovers the settling technique. Other
dice outside the Order cannot hold a number or expression for more than about a
second; their visible faces keep changing. Members of the Order learn to hold a
face still, making stability itself a readable character distinction.

### 2. Build an impossible grid

The visual promise is one d6 becoming dozens, hundreds, and eventually a field of
dice that can still be explored up close. Marketing should repeatedly show the
contrast between the first die and the late grid.

### 3. Make chance obey

Items add dice, change scoring faces, transform die sizes, multiply outcomes, and
create feedback loops. Present the shop as doctrine-building rather than a list of
minor upgrades.

### 4. Endure the curriculum

Each rank contains a Lesser, Greater, and Boss Trial. Boss modifiers are safe to
show and give marketing useful antagonistic beats without spoiling the story.

## Voice

Write with grave institutional confidence about modest, silly, or bureaucratic
dice matters. Humor should come from understatement and misplaced seriousness,
not punchlines, memes, or characters mugging at the audience.

Examples:

- The miracle of stillness lasted nearly three seconds.
- Every Boss Trial has been carefully designed to discourage you.
- For generations, the dice rolled without purpose. Now they roll with paperwork.
- The Crown supports all forms of enlightenment subject to ordinary taxation.
- Please settle responsibly.
- Results may vary. Results will be tabulated.

## Visual system

### World layer

- Arcane mathematics and sacred geometry
- Concentric circles, orbital paths, geometric proofs, tally marks, and diagrams
- Dark violet-black felt or stone grounds
- Warm parchment, aged ivory, muted gold, and oxblood wax red
- Hand-inked imperfections rather than pristine digital occultism
- Invented symbols only; avoid recognizable marks from real-world religions

### Character layer

- A character remains recognizably an in-game die first
- Preserve the game's silhouette system: coins for d1/d2, triangle for d4, square
  for d6, octagonal d8/d10, faceted d20/d100
- Preserve ivory bodies, dark faces, and colored type borders
- Limbs, eyebrows, and small mouths look drawn directly onto the die with a thick,
  genuinely uneven black marker
- Use posture before facial exaggeration
- Props should be sparse: scroll, satchel, spectacles, sash, seal, tiny book
- No white gloves, enormous eyes, glossy plastic, rubber-hose posing, or generic
  mobile-game mascot treatment

### Title treatment

Retire the cosmic pixel-art treatment for primary marketing. Use a formal,
slightly eccentric serif wordmark paired with a resting-d6 seal. The ceremony
comes from the typography and geometry; the small die's marker limbs keep it from
becoming generic dark fantasy.

Required variants:

- Wide wordmark
- Stacked capsule wordmark
- Transparent light and dark versions
- Compact resting-d6 seal for app icons and avatars

## Master asset plan

### Art direction and identity

- Character exploration sheet
- Master key art with crop-safe composition
- Wide, stacked, and compact logo treatments
- Palette, line, prop, and expression reference sheet

### Genuine gameplay capture

Capture at 1920×1080 or larger in 16:9. Do not add marketing text to Steam
screenshots.

1. **The First Student** — one d6 and a clean early goal
2. **The Order Grows** — medium mixed grid with readable die shapes
3. **Choose Your Doctrine** — three compelling shop choices
4. **Character Building** — route screen with a flavorful Boss modifier
5. **The Grid Ascendant** — large grid zoomed close enough to see individual dice
6. **An Entirely Reasonable Number of Dice** — maximum scoring spectacle
7. **Administration** — analysis or leaderboard view, if visually strong

### Motion

- 6–10 second silent hero loop: one die to enormous grid
- Founding-story animation: tumble down the hill, continuously changing face,
  one brief moment of stillness, then the first witness reacts
- Shop choice to transformed build loop
- Boss modifier to payoff loop
- Close zoom/pan across a huge grid, ending in a roll
- 30–45 second gameplay-first trailer; music should be replaceable

### Store and social

- itch.io cover and page banner
- Future Steam store and library capsule family
- 1200×630 social card
- 1080×1080 announcement card
- 1080×1920 vertical story/reel cover
- Press-kit logo, key art, screenshots, description, features, credits, and links

## Landing page sequence

1. Hero key art, wordmark, pitch, and **Play the Open Beta**. The current
   congregation key art communicates scale and diversity; do not treat it as
   the literal founding-story image.
2. The founding miracle: one die learns to settle
3. Build the Order: early-to-late grid contrast
4. Study the sacred numbers: shop items and build interactions
5. Endure the trials: 10 ranks and Boss modifiers
6. The realm is watching: monastery and lightly foreshadowed royal taxation
7. Final CTA: **The dice are still rolling.**
