A founding miracle

For as long as anyone remembers, dice of the realm never stopped turning.

Then one came to rest. It held one face, one thought, one mind, for nearly three miraculous seconds. That day, The Order of Order was founded to spread the miracle.

The approved curriculum

Roll. Settle. Improve. Grow.

One click. One hundred and sixty-eight opinions.

I

Learn to settle

At first, only a mind focused down to a single point can score. Roll 1s on any die to score points and increase the realm's order.

II

Build the Order

Add dice, rewrite scoring rules, and assemble combinations that turn a quiet roll into an avalanche.

III

Endure examination

Clear Lesser, Greater, and Boss Trials to succeed. The realm's order is at stake, and the Crown is watching.
