The Order of Order

An incremental rite of dice.

Roll ones. Survive the thresholds. Save the world.

The Order of Order is a dice-building incremental roguelike about turning one humble d6 into a screen-filling engine of chance. Every point keeps you alive—but those same points are the only currency accepted by the shop. Spend too much and miss the round's target. Spend too little and your build may never grow strong enough for what comes next.

What Awaits Within

Build a Dice Empire

Grow from a single die to dozens, hundreds, or far beyond. d1, d2, d4, d6, d8, d10, d20, and d100 dice each have their own readable shape.

Break the Scoring Rules

Score matching faces, load or shrink whole die sizes, add wild faces, multiply every point, and discover combos that turn a quiet roll into an avalanche.

45 Shop Items

Common, uncommon, and rare relics create wildly different runs—from disciplined small-die builds to explosive grids that reproduce whenever they score.

Chasing the Score

Every round raises the score you must reach to survive. Grow your engine, chase the next threshold, and carry your run through all 10 escalating rounds.

Permanent Discoveries

Complete feats during a run to unlock new items. Track every discovery, unlock condition, and purchase count inside the Codex.

Built for Replays

Ten escalating rounds, randomized shops, dynamic prices, a global leaderboard, persistent progression, and a harder mode waiting beyond your first victory.

The Shop

Every price is a wager against the score you still need to survive.

How to Perform the Rite

  1. Roll the dice. A die showing a sacred scoring number earns points. At first, that number is 1.

  2. Watch the target. Each round gives you 20 rolls to reach the displayed score threshold.

  3. Visit the shop. After rolls 5 and 15, trade some of your score for new dice, extra scoring faces, multipliers, rule-changing relics, and stranger powers.

  4. Survive the reckoning. Reach the target by the final roll or the run ends. Clear the round and your score resets—unless your build has found a way around that.

  5. Clear all 10 rounds. The final threshold is waiting. Defeat it to restore order—and unlock an even harsher challenge.

Controls: Use a mouse or touchscreen. Press the Roll button, tap cards to buy them, and select dice when an item asks for a target. Zoom in and out, then pan around the board to explore even the most enormous dice grids.
